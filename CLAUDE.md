# Buffr Intelligence — project rules

Standing rules for this project. Workspace-wide rules live in the parent
`CLAUDE.md` and still apply; this file only adds what is specific here.

**Read [`buffr-intelligence.md`](buffr-intelligence.md) first** — it is the
brief. Detail lives in [`docs/`](docs/README.md), and
[`changelog.md`](changelog.md) is the dated record of what was actually built.

---

## 1. What this is

A RegTech and SupTech analytics platform for the Bank of Namibia. It reads
transaction-pattern data from Namibia's instant payment rails and produces
dashboards, explainable anomaly detection, market-structure and inclusion
measures, and regulatory returns.

**There is no device, no firmware, no hardware, and no consumer product.** An
earlier version of this repository was built around a payment-confirmation
speaker; none of it remains, and nothing should reintroduce it. If a doc,
comment or component still refers to devices, firmware, sellers or a merchant
portal, that is a leftover to remove rather than a feature to support.

## 2. The one fact everything else rests on

**Observer, not participant.** The platform is told the outcome of payments
that have already settled and analyses them. It cannot start, stop, hold or
reverse a payment, and it holds no funds at any point.

This is structural. There is no HTTP client in `backend/app/` that calls a
payment rail, and no function named initiate, authorise, debit, decline, hold
or reverse. **Do not add one.** If a task appears to require moving money,
stop and raise it — it means the task has been misunderstood.

## 3. Vocabulary that carries meaning

- **Participant, not merchant.** Individuals transact in their own right;
  person-to-person is a live use case. Say *business* only where a business is
  specifically meant.
- **Anomaly, not fraud.** The system scores unusual activity. Only a reviewer's
  recorded verdict is a statement about fraud, and `confirmed_fraud` /
  `not_fraud` are the only places the word belongs.
- **Region, constituency and town are three different things.** 14 regions,
  121 official constituencies. Local authorities are a separate classification
  under the Local Authorities Act, not a subdivision. Coverage reports at
  constituency level because that is where a gap is visible.
- **Two roles only:** regulator and admin.

## 4. Honesty rules, which are product requirements here

- **No shared data exists.** Everything runs on `scripts/seed_synthetic.py`.
  Any figure shown anywhere — site, docs, notebook — must say so. A chart that
  looks real is the one that most needs the caveat.
- **Nothing is marked live that is not running.** Capability that is designed
  but not running is labelled as being built.
- **Absent is null, never zero.** A region never measured and a region with no
  activity are different findings. Do not merge them.
- **Every ratio carries its denominator.** A rate over eleven payments is
  arithmetic; over eleven thousand it is evidence.
- **Refuse rather than estimate.** Where the data cannot answer, the system
  says so — the forecaster returns nothing under four weeks of history, and
  the assistant declines questions its reviewed functions cannot compute.
- **No endorsement implied.** WayaMe and any institution are named to say what
  the platform observes. No logo, claim or phrasing may suggest a partnership,
  licence or approval that has not been granted.

## 6. Public pages

- **Every section carries a real visual.** A page of text boxes describing
  analytics is the least convincing way to present analytics.
- **Charts show our own computed output**, framed as measurements with a
  monospace label and a required source line.
- **No stock photography, no trusted-by logos, no live counters.** The first
  is filler; the other two would require inventing customers or a data feed
  that does not exist.
- Four pages: Landing, For regulators, How it works, Privacy.
